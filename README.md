# The Dunes Intred
This game is inspired by Dungeons & Dragons (D&D). It is a dungeon-crawling RPG that runs right in your browser. You can sign up and log in whenever you want, and all your in-game data is kept, so you can come back and keep playing whenever you want!

Fight monsters and bosses across 100 areas, level up, collect loot, craft gear and potions, learn spells, unlock titles and achievements, customize your avatar, show off your profile and trade items with other players.

## Requirements
* Any web browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).
* That's it! No Python, no installing, nothing else needed.

## How to play on any PC
### Download and play
1. On this page, click the green **`<> Code`** button near the top, then click **Download ZIP**.
2. Find `Dunes-Intred-2-main.zip` in your Downloads folder. Right-click it, choose **Extract All…**, then click **Extract**.
3. Open the extracted `Dunes-Intred-2-main` folder and double-click **`index.html`**. The game opens in your browser.

Keep all the files together: `index.html` needs the `css` and `js` folders next to it, so don't move it out of the folder on its own. Tip: right-click `index.html` → **Send to → Desktop (create shortcut)** to start the game straight from your desktop.

### Play online
No download needed: **https://raihan-s-mohammed.github.io/Dunes-Intred-2/**

## Your save data
* Your progress saves automatically in the browser you play in, on that PC.
* Playing in a different browser or on a different PC starts fresh. To move your progress, use **Settings → Export save** on the old one and **Settings → Import save** on the new one.

## Controls
* Everything has buttons, but in battle you can also use the keyboard: `A` attack, `M` magic (then `1`–`4`), `D` dodge, `P` items, `R` run.
* `E` explores the dungeon.
